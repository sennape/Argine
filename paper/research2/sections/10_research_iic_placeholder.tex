\section{Research II-C -- Causal Prediction and Runtime Validation (Placeholder)}
\label{sec:iic-placeholder}

\textit{Research II-C has not started. This section states only its
intended scope; it reports no finding, result, or claim.}

Research~II-C is intended to evaluate whether strictly causal policies can
improve on selected causal baselines and to measure, under preregistered
semantics, how much of the measured II-A Belady comparator gap they recover
(Definition~\ref{def:ii-c-metric}, Eq.~\ref{eq:ii-c}). Because
\textsc{AUDIT-ORACLE-001} (CASE~C) establishes that $O_B$ is not proven
byte-optimal and that $O_B$ and $C^*$ have no guaranteed ordering,
recovery relative to $O_B$ is a comparator-relative metric, not a proven
fraction of maximum causal opportunity. The planned boundary is:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{R2-09 -- Prediction Benchmark.}
   \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{R2-09A Causal Baseline Tournament:} evaluate causal
      retention baselines under a leakage-safe selection contract. R2-09A
      begins from existing canonical DEV evidence (Global-LRU, LFU, and the
      existing R2-03 capacity sweep, where LFU is canonical auxiliary
      evidence) and extends the causal baseline frontier to additional
      preregistered families such as 2Q, LRU-K, aged/decayed frequency, and
      other precisely defined causal baselines. Existing LFU DEV evidence
      does not replace R2-09A: R2-09A must still establish leakage-safe
      DEV-only tuning, freeze policy hyperparameters, compare additional
      causal baseline families, establish held-out EVAL reporting, and
      determine $B_\mathrm{best}$ under the future benchmark contract.
      Select $B_\mathrm{best}$ on DEV only before held-out EVAL reporting.
    \item \textbf{R2-09B Benchmark Contract / preregistration:} freeze
      DEV/EVAL roles and held-out EVAL acquisition/design; baseline
      selection without EVAL leakage; comparator semantics ($O_B$ unless a
      valid $O^*$ bound is established); deterministic/stochastic
      evaluation conditions; metric definitions; EXP-025 applicability;
      practical-effect threshold derivation; and falsification criteria.
   \end{itemize}
 \item \textbf{R2-10 -- Causal Working-Set Predictor:} build and evaluate
   strictly causal predictors (no future information) under the frozen
   R2-09B contract. R2-09 contains no final R2-10 predictor result.
 \item \textbf{R2-11 -- Prediction-Driven Retention:} evaluate, in replay,
   whether predictor-driven retention improves on $B_\mathrm{best}$ and
   report comparator-relative recovery (Eq.~\ref{eq:ii-c}) and
   $\mathrm{Recovery\_vs\_LRU}$ where useful. Values $>1$ for
   $\mathrm{RecoveryOfBeladyGap}$ are possible only as comparator-relative
   outcomes and do not imply beating $O^*$.
 \item \textbf{R2-12 -- Real Runtime Validation:} validate any
   predictive-retention result against the real runtime, not only offline
   replay. Modeled EXP-025 quantities are not measured end-to-end latency,
   time-to-first answer, or throughput.
\end{enumerate}

No experiment in this list has been run, and no value for
$\mathrm{RecoveryOfBeladyGap}_\budget$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:ii-c}) has been
computed. The signed comparator-relative recovery metric (relative to the
measured comparator gap) remains \notyetmeasured{}.
