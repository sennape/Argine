\subsection{Oracle-semantics scientific amendment}
\textsc{AUDIT-ORACLE-001} (commit \texttt{3789a8511e9623f953dda5a610c9d7897e4ae63b},
CASE~C, \texttt{BLOCKED\_FOR\_SCIENTIFIC\_AMENDMENT}) found that the
implemented \textsc{Oracle-Global-Belady} policy is a clairvoyant
Belady-style farthest-next-use comparator ($O_B$, algorithm class O2), not
proven byte-optimal under the variable-size, byte-capacity miss-byte
objective. The paper amendment preserves all R2-01--R2-08 numerical
evidence, run IDs, digests, trace hashes, and data-derived figures/tables;
it corrects semantics (title, definitions, comparator-gap framing,
recovery-metric interpretation, limitations, DEV/EVAL/EXP-025 transparency,
and II-C roadmap). The distinct amendment provenance record is
\texttt{ORACLE-SEMANTICS-AMENDMENT.json}; historical
\texttt{II-A-FREEZE.json} and \texttt{II-B-FREEZE.json} are preserved as
freeze-time records and are not silently rewritten.
