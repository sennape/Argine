\subsection{R-08 capture-ID audit}
\label{sec:r08-capture-audit}

The reused \code{coding-v1/seed-42} cell of the R-08 matrix carries the run
identifier of the upstream R-01 capture,
\mbox{\hsh{bb6d8ca34dbc4537a43d54be30c9f3e1}}, together with its capture source,
while the seven R-08-managed new cells carry a null run identifier and a
null capture source. This is an intentional provenance representation, not
a missing scientific trace: every canonical cell still records a trace
path, a trace digest, its workload identity and seed, the model and runtime
digests, the configuration fingerprint, its validation state, and its
canonical status, and every cell is bound to the canonical R-08 run
\mbox{\hsh{6165471a12574e05b48932aac2f1626c}}. No capture identifier was invented
and no scientific trace was modified for this publication.

\subsection{Oracle-semantics scientific amendment}

\textsc{AUDIT-ORACLE-001} (commit
\hsh{3789a8511e9623f953dda5a610c9d7897e4ae63b}, CASE~C, recorded under
the blocking-scientific-amendment status) established that the
implemented \textsc{Oracle-Global-Belady} policy is a clairvoyant
Belady-style farthest-next-use comparator ($O_B$, algorithm class O2), not
proven byte-optimal under the variable-size, byte-capacity miss-byte
objective. The amendment preserved every R-01--R-08 numerical result, run
identifier, digest, trace hash, and data-derived figure and table; it
corrected semantics only: the title, the definitions, the comparator-gap
framing, the recovery-metric interpretation, the limitations, and the
DEV/EVAL/EXP-025 transparency. The amendment is recorded as a distinct
provenance record rather than by silently rewriting the frozen records that
precede it.

\subsection{Freeze and amendment records}

preserved unchanged, including their stage-level freeze manifests. The
later stage is frozen in the repository-level closure record for Research~2
and in the manuscript-level freeze and provenance records stored with this
paper. Those records are machine-readable and are not reproduced in the
printed text; this appendix is their human-readable counterpart, and
Table~\ref{tab:hash-registry} is the authoritative printed list of complete
digests. This document is the complete, frozen publication of the research
programme it reports.
