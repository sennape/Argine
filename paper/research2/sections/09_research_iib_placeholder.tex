\section{Research II-B -- Routing Characterization (Placeholder)}
\label{sec:iib-placeholder}

\textit{Research II-B has not started. This section states only its
intended scope; it reports no finding, result, or claim.}

The central question II-A leaves open is: \emph{what structure in expert
routing explains the large observed policy-information headroom
(Section~\ref{sec:recoverable-envelope}), and is that structure causally
exploitable?} Research II-B is intended to investigate four component
questions, corresponding to planned experiments R2-05--R2-08:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Expert frequency and concentration} (R2-05): is expert
   access frequency concentrated across layers, or diffuse?
 \item \textbf{Routing transition structure} (R2-06): are transitions
   between experts across steps structured (e.g.\ Markovian), or
   effectively unstructured?
 \item \textbf{Reuse-distance structure} (R2-07): what reuse-distance
   distribution explains the Oracle's advantage over causal LRU-family
   policies?
 \item \textbf{Workload/routing drift} (R2-08): does any structure found
   in R2-05--R2-07 persist across workload phases, or across different
   workloads?
\end{enumerate}
No experiment in this list has been run. Any numeric finding attributed
to II-B prior to the publication of a corresponding II-B report should be
treated as unsupported.
