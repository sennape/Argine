## Historical-sequencing note (explicit, per closure instructions)

The literal historical statement **"R2-11 NOT PROPOSED under the original frozen R2-10 decision rules"** remains true and unchanged: under `experiments/R2-10-working-set-predictor/decision_rules.json`'s original Category A/B/C criteria (which are textually anchored to the DEV corpus), Category B evidence alone does not provide a path to proposing R2-11. R2-BRIDGE-01 is represented, correctly, as **prospective evidence generated under a new, separate, later-frozen contract** that subsequently justified proposing R2-11 as its own protocol — not as a retroactive satisfaction or reversal of the R2-10 decision rule's original text. Both statements are true simultaneously and are not in tension.
