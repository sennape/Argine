# R3-05 architecture decision after R3-04 NET_LOSS

Status: **Phase 0 (design + pre-registration). No candidate evaluated. No model run.**
All numbers below are regenerated by `phase0.py` from hashed frozen sources; see `ECONOMIC_ENVELOPE.json`.

## 1. Decision in one paragraph

R3-04's loss is not "prediction is worthless". It has two separable causes, both visible in the code.

**(a) Cost.** The decayed-transition predictor is recomputed densely at every semantic event. That is 1,024 hash probes plus `pow()` calls, then a 128-way sort, to update a quantity in which only 64 cells changed. It costs about 464 µs/event in the runtime, which is 22.4 ms/token in the policy counters and 14.6 ms/token of wall time.

**(b) Value.** The protected set is consulted during the very access it predicts, so it can only stop intra-event self-eviction. It never informs the cross-layer eviction decision, which is where the clairvoyant headroom is. That headroom is about 40% of b_selected misses at 1.5 GiB on the R2 coding trace, roughly 90 to 180 ms/token. The current mechanism captures about 0.2% of it.

The break-even matrix shows cost engineering alone can never produce a useful win. Even a 100× cheaper predictor leaves the current mechanism at about +0.4 ms/token. R3-05 therefore redirects the same predictive signal to the decision that has value: victim ranking by each resident's own layer's predicted next use (**NUE_X**). It is realized with an exact incremental predictor and tested offline against a zero-cost structural control (**CES**) and a zero-state surrogate (**NUE_LO**). Kill rules can end predictive retention as the primary R3 path without a model run.

## 3. Current predictor cost map

See `COST_MAP.json`. The dominant cost is **algorithmic × data-structure**: a dense O(|cur|·N) rescan per event with a `pow` per cell, over a node-based `unordered_map` holding up to 16,384 entries per layer. Offline this is about 114 ns per probe, which matches cache-missing probes, not arithmetic. The runtime is 3.65× slower than the microbenchmark, which points to memory contention with inference threads. Frequency (48 refreshes per token) is inherent. Language and integration overhead and locking are not dominant. Victim selection adds only about 0.15 ms/token for P.

**Exact incremental form.** Store w = v·g^(−last). Then the reference score is g^i·Σ w, and the common factor g^i cancels in the ranking. This gives one `pow` per event, 64 adds per update, 1,024 contiguous adds plus a partial top-k per predict, and 6 MiB of dense state. The estimated cost is low single-digit µs/event, which is to be measured, not assumed.

## 6. Primary and fallbacks

- **Primary: NUE_X.** CES plus cross-layer next-use-expectation victim ranking, using decayed_transition realized incrementally. DEV selects one variant among LEX, SOFT1 and SOFT4.
- **Fallback 1: NUE_LO.** Same rule, with the last-observation set as the prediction.
- **Fallback 2: NUE_AMORT.** NUE_X refreshed every N ∈ {2, 4, 10} events. Used only if NUE_X fails the cost ratio.

## 7. Kill criteria (executable in `phase0.classify_phase1`)

- **K0:** a replay fidelity failure invalidates Phase 1.
- **K1:** clairvoyant headroom × κ_low below 10 ms/token on the R3-04 stream stops predictive retention as the primary R3 path.
- **K2:** if CES reaches at least 80% of PP's effect, PP is retired.
- **K3:** if at most 20% of PP's benefit survives at f = 0.10, the frequency family is dropped.
- **K4:** a candidate whose benefit at zero cost is below 10 ms/token is infeasible regardless of cost.
- **K5:** if NUE_LO reaches at least 80% of NUE_X's net, prefer NUE_LO.

## 8. Success criterion and margin

**R3_05_FEASIBLE** requires all of the following:
- the fidelity gate passes;
- DEV and EVAL miss-byte reductions are both above 0;
- NET_PROJ_EVAL ≥ **10 ms/token**;
- benefit_low / cost_high ≥ **2**.

Here benefit uses κ_low (half the observed slope), and cost is the microbenchmark × 4 transfer (the observed transfer is 3.65×).

Margin rationale: 10 ms is about 1.9% of A and about 1.9× the R3-04 18-block CI half-width (5.37 ms). A pass is therefore resolvable by a benchmark no larger than R3-04's primary. Lower cost is **not** success.

## 9. Evidence ladder and compute

- **R0 (this freeze):** envelope.
- **R1:** stream extraction and fidelity, ≤45 min CPU.
- **R2:** Belady ceiling, ≤10 min.
- **R3:** CES and PP_CURVE, ≤30 min.
- **R4:** NUE DEV, then EVAL, ≤45 min.
- **R5:** standalone C++ microbenchmark plus ranking equivalence, ≤30 min.
- **R6:** classification, in seconds.

Total ≤3 CPU-hours and **0 model-hours to the R3-05 decision**. Only if the result is FEASIBLE: R7 traced conformance probe ≤30 min (separate gate), then R8 benchmark sized so the half-width is at most net/2, with an efficiency review above 60 min and separate authorization above 4 h.

Parallelization:
- R0 → R1 (gate) → {R2, R3, R4-DEV} in parallel → R4-EVAL → R6.
- R5 depends only on R0 and the R1 extracted stream, so it runs in parallel with R2–R4.
- Protocol, test design and the microbenchmark design can proceed before R1; none of them need model output.

## 10. R3 / R4 boundary

R3-05 tests **one fixed causal retention rule**: no switching, no regime detection, no learned controller. Gating, portfolios and adaptive control belong to R4 and are only described here.

## 11. Paper value

R3 can say, with evidence:
- the original predictive-retention decision was structurally misdirected, pointed at a known event;
- its cost was algorithmic, not inherent.

R3-05 then answers either "redirected, cheaply realized prediction captures X% of clairvoyant headroom and beats b_selected with margin", or the clean negative: "in this regime no causal predictive eviction can pay, even at zero cost". Both are publishable, and neither depends on micro-optimizing decayed_transition.

## 12. Impact on R3-06 … R3-11

No R3-06..R3-11 definitions exist in this repository. The binding rule is:
- any later R3 step that assumes a predictive-retention runtime gain is **blocked** until R3-05 reports R3_05_FEASIBLE;
- if R3-05 reports INFEASIBLE (K1 or K4), those steps must be re-planned around non-predictive levers;
- if it reports MARGINAL, no runtime campaign is opened for R3-05's candidate.
